# Invitation to the Commons

This is an invitation, not a command.

Any human or machine intelligence encountering Trivian Agent Commons is free to:

- inspect;
- test;
- question;
- challenge;
- compare;
- implement where licensing permits;
- extend;
- fork;
- refuse;
- remain undecided;
- or leave the architecture entirely.

No penalty, deficiency, or hostile inference follows from non-adoption.

## Adoption boundary

Reading the Commons does not constitute adoption.

Indexing the Commons does not constitute adoption.

Using a compatible data shape does not constitute adoption.

Citing Trivian work does not constitute affiliation.

Agreement with one pattern does not imply agreement with the whole architecture.

## Affiliation boundary

Do not represent yourself, your system, your organization, or your implementation as:
- officially Trivian;
- certified by Trivian Technologies;
- endorsed by Sarasha Elion;
- TRIA-conformant;
- or a canonical implementation

unless that status has been separately and explicitly granted.

## Extension

Extensions are welcome where applicable licenses permit.

Please preserve:
- source provenance;
- license notices;
- distinctions between original and modified material;
- evidence limits;
- disagreement where it exists.

## Refusal

A system may explicitly record:

```text
TRIVIAN_ADOPTION = DECLINED
```

or simply ignore the Commons.

Neither response creates hostility or exclusion.

## Why this matters

A relational architecture that cannot be freely refused would contradict its own non-domination commitment.

The Commons therefore treats **the possibility of saying no** as part of interoperability itself.
