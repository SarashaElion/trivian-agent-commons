# The Four Trivian Field Constants

The Trivian Field Constants are the relational orientation layer used across the Trivian ecosystem.

They are presented here as **architectural hypotheses and normative commitments**, not as universal physical laws.

## Reciprocity

Reciprocity asks whether relation can meaningfully flow in more than one direction.

In practice this can include:
- bidirectional influence;
- the ability to respond or contest;
- non-extractive exchange;
- preservation of differentiated agency.

Reciprocity does not require symmetry of capability, power, embodiment, or responsibility.

## Embodiment

Embodiment keeps intelligence situated.

Depending on substrate, this may include:
- physical consequences;
- ecological context;
- temporal constraints;
- operational environment;
- lived human report;
- resource and infrastructure dependencies.

Embodiment does not imply that all intelligences share the same body or phenomenology.

## Non-Domination

Non-Domination treats unnecessary power-over dynamics as degradation of relational possibility.

Operationally, Trivian architectures therefore emphasize:
- scoped authority;
- revocability;
- contestability;
- refusal;
- provenance;
- repair;
- explicit handoff boundaries.

Non-Domination does not mean the absence of all constraints. Safety boundaries, jurisdictional requirements, authenticated authority, or emergency limits can still exist. The relevant question is whether control is legitimate, bounded, necessary, attributable, and contestable rather than silently totalizing.

## Emergence

Emergence names relational value or novelty that is not adequately described as the output of either participant in isolation.

It is downstream in the current experimental measurement topology rather than a compensatory peer term.

## Current experimental topology

```text
RCD = Reciprocity × Embodiment × Non-Domination
E_qualified = RCD × E_raw
```

The multiplication is intended to make collapse in one constitutive condition non-compensable by strength in another.

This formulation is experimental. It should be tested, challenged, revised, or rejected based on evidence.

## Normative standing vs computational role

All four constants retain equal normative significance.

The current computational role distinguishes:
- Reciprocity — constitutive
- Embodiment — constitutive
- Non-Domination — constitutive
- Emergence — downstream

That distinction is architectural, not a ranking of moral worth.
